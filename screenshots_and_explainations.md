## Suspect Information & Observations

![Suspect Information](screenshots/screenshot(1).png)

### Explanation

This screen allows investigators to enter structured suspect information,
including the suspect name or alias, age, observed behaviors, methods of
operation (MO), and relevant traits.

The *Run Intelligence Analysis* feature is designed to process these
observations using AI and transform raw investigative information into
structured intelligence and actionable insights.

### Future Prospects

- AI-powered behavioral profiling
- Correlation with previous cases and evidence
- Multi-agent investigation and analysis
- Investigation risk and priority assessment
- Automatic case timeline generation
- AI-assisted investigative report generation
- Secure case management with authentication and audit logs
